v3.8
Set the vibe figure in Inter, at the same size and weight as the walk, taxi and subway times. It was the one number on the card with no font stack of its own, so it inherited Plus Jakarta Sans — whose numerals are distinctive enough to read as a display face next to Inter figures.
v3.7
Sort control is taller and carries icons — a pin, a dollar sign, a cocktail glass. Cards were rebalanced: the subway routing line came off (it lives in the popup), vibe and reviews moved onto a line of their own, and the Est. rate chip moved into the right-hand column beneath the figures it qualifies, rather than sitting among the room details.
v3.6
Vibe is now a cocktail glass and a number — the five-pip scale is gone. The pips spent a lot of width restating a figure already printed beside them. Also dropped the room-inventory counts; “10+ rooms” was true of almost every property and told you nothing. Scarcity still surfaces where it matters, on the one hotel down to seven.
v3.5
Labelled the vibe score. The cards showed a bare number and five pips with nothing saying what they measured — now they read Vibe 4.3, with a hover breakdown of the cool / architecture / scene sub-scores. The popup block is retitled Vibe — my subjective read, since “Ratings” implied it came from somewhere authoritative.
v3.4
Removed the Book in Andavo hand-off added in v3.2. traveler-web reads only ?tab= and ?tripId=, so the link could only ever open an empty hotel search — no destination, no dates, no property. A link that strands you is worse than no link. It comes back when a URL can carry the search.
v3.3
Map and list are now two views of one selection. Picking a dot highlights its card and scrolls the list to it; picking a card lights the dot — the same state drives both. Selection is also a real state now, distinct from hover: hovering stays a light preview, while the selected card gets an accent rail, a ring and a tinted ground, and survives re-sorting.
v3.2
Added a Book in Andavo hand-off per hotel, opening the hotel search tab at app.andavo.com behind sign-in. It deliberately does not claim to land on the property: traveler-web has no property route, and mobile’s is keyed to an ephemeral offer id from a live search — so no stable per-hotel link exists to point at yet. Styled quieter than the Reviews link for the same reason.
v3.1
Every hotel now links out to its Google Maps listing for real reviews — on the card and in the popup. Deliberately a link rather than an embedded score: a number baked into the page goes stale the day it ships, and the rating sits next to the vibe score so the crowd read and the subjective read are visibly different things.

Their rates are provisional estimates that have not been pulled, and are marked as such throughout: a dashed Est. rate chip, a ~ prefix, dashed underlines on the figures, and a dashed ring on the map marker.
